🛒 Point of Sales (POS) System
📖 Overview

Point of Sales (POS) is a complete inventory and sales management system designed to help businesses efficiently manage their products, sales operations, and user access.
It provides a user-friendly dashboard, real-time POS screen, and advanced analytics for business insights.
Whether you’re running a small shop or a large retail store, this system simplifies your workflow by centralizing product, category, and user management — all in one place.

🚀 Features
💰 Sales & POS

Intuitive POS screen for quick transactions
Add items to cart and process payments instantly
Track ongoing and completed transactions
Support for multiple payment methods

📦 Product Management

Add, update, and delete products
Manage stock levels and minimum stock alerts
Associate products with categories for better organization

🧩 Category Management

Create and manage product categories
Assign images and names to categories for easy identification

👥 User Management

Manage user accounts and roles
Secure authentication and authorization system
Separate access for admins and sales staff

📊 Dashboard & Analytics

View total sales, revenue, and top-selling products
Track inventory levels and user performance
Interactive charts and metrics for business analysis
